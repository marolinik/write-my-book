Morning. No letters.

I took the gull road instead of the steps, the gull road is longer, the gull road does not care that it is longer. Neither did I.

Grey water, grey light, grey wind off a grey sea, and the fifth grey is the one inside your chest, the one the others are only weather compared to.

Halfway down there is a bench my father set into the rock. Not for rest. For watching. He said a keeper who sits with her back to the sea is a townswoman on holiday, he said it every time, he said it the last time, I sat with my back to the sea.

Small rebellions. The only size I ever managed.

The Morena's mast shows at low tide off Struga. A finger of her. Pointing at nothing, pointing at everything, depends on the tide, depends on the sister. I watched until the water took the finger back.

Kova says salvage rights lapse in a year and a day. A year and a day. Like a sentence in an old story, like a curse with paperwork.

I came back up by the steps. Ninety-six. I counted them the way I always count them, which is to say I did not count them, my feet counted them, my feet have been right for thirty years.

Wind rising by evening. Pressure falling. Wrote it down. Drew no line.

Let the night try the door.
