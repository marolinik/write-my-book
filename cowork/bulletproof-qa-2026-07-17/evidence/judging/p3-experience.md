## 2. Defects I found in the evidence (missed or under-called by the executor)

1. **Event-name forking predates the T4 seeds — duplicate death events in the original canon extraction.** Baseline trace `21_neo4j_baseline_book1.txt` lines 15+21 show BOTH "Death of Corvin Ashe" (fA 3.0) and "Corvin Ashe's Death" (fA 5.0) from the ordinary ch1-5 run — ch5's truce retelling forked a duplicate death event before any adversarial seeding. D-32(c) is pervasive in normal writing, not an artifact of the timeline seeds. Never flagged. Severity: S2-corroborating.
2. **User-facing sidebar copy is chapter-relative and overwrite-churned.** The `+=` overwrite semantics behind D-27 also wholesale-replace `description`/`role`/`personality`: Corvin's description reads "died one month prior to this chapter" (written from Book 1 ch5's viewpoint) and is served verbatim to the Book 2 writer (09b), where "this chapter" is meaningless and grows staler forever; his role churns mentioned→protagonist across re-extractions (21 vs 25). Executor filed the aliases case only. Severity: S3, user-visible.
3. **D-27 already leaks to the user surface.** 09b's sidebar payload shows `aliases:["Zoe"]` for Zoë — the regressed array is not just internal graph state; the "alias-matched" guarantee is visibly degraded in the product surface. Executor treated it as graph-level only. S4/note.
4. **9.6 s synchronous [Intentional] response** (trace 19, `elapsedMs: 9578`) unremarked — an interactive suppress action taking ~10 s (dev, single sample). S3-observation.
5. **Evidence-process gap: manuscripts absent from the bundle.** Full prose lives in scratchpad/doc-store ("Full prose = scratchpad ch6.md", trace 27); only ch7's bytes plus first-80-char snippets are preserved. A judge cannot independently verify the seeded prose says what's claimed (e.g., ch6's "keeps him explicitly dead"). Not a product defect; weakens auditability of T2/T4 claims.
6. **Worker-proof is adequate for 2026-07-18 but not contemporaneous for the predecessor's 2026-07-17 measurements** (`worker-proof.txt` captured 07-18; single-worker for 07-17 rests on PID-continuity inference with P1's capture). The proof also concedes extraction runs in-process in the dev server, with "one in flight at a time" resting on executor discipline. I do not VOID the 07-18 measurements (worker count proven, dev-labeled, and the load-bearing claims are landing/not-landing facts, not throughput); 07-17-only timing claims I treat as weak — none are score-bearing.
7. **Cross-tenant claim in D-30 is structural, not empirical.** Proven same-user cross-book; no second-tenant fixture with colliding character names was run. The S1-candidate framing is reasonable but the tenant half remains code-inferred.
