## 3. "Suspiciously clean" analysis

This bundle mostly earns trust by being conspicuously *unclean*: it preserves its own FAILs (5 kept in `_results.json` with explanations), self-reports two test-methodology false-positive episodes with root-cause dumps, documents an environment blocker, a MAX_PATH tooling failure, and its own CRLF comparison blunder, and explicitly declines to claim Z11 live. That is the opposite of curated gloss. What *is* suspiciously absent: (a) any attempt to fix or re-verify D-16, the worst finding filed; (b) the entire manuscript-intelligence moat — the product's claimed differentiator — is 100% unexercised in the bundle handed to a manuscript-intelligence judge, and the one agent digest that leaked in shows zero findings; (c) every request rides the `x-e2e-test-secret` auth bypass, so no real auth/ownership surface was ever touched; (d) all UI verification of the things actually tested (unicode rendering, version-history UI, find/replace UI, export progress) is deferred; (e) no worker-proof discipline despite the campaign clearly knowing about stale-worker hazards. The clean 3×-green re-verify tables (D-03, D-23/D-24) are credible because the original failure evidence sits beside them — but note both re-verifies were executed by the same parties who wrote the fixes' evidence trail.
