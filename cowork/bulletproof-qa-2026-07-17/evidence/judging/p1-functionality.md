## 3. "Suspiciously clean" analysis

Executor-proposed grades were not adopted; where they were harsh (D8 FAIL pre-fix) the raw data supports them, and where they were rosy (FIXED-VERIFIED framings) I discounted.

## 4. One-line overall impression

A product with a real, byte-verifiably honest editorial moat (19/19 verbatim anchors, real memory persistence, honest cost/stat surfaces) wrapped in an unreliable delivery layer — silent empty replies, duplicate-finding churn, crash-and-retry tool calls, and metadata that occasionally lies — i.e., a strong engine that a paying debut novelist would still experience as flaky.
