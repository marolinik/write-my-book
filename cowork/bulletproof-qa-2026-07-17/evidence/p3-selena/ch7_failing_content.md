The alliance lasted eleven days.

Mira Thorne learned from her own scouts that Kestrel Vane's raiders — the very ones he had sworn were not his — had been spotted meeting openly with the "unbannered" raider fleet at a cove south of Duskhaven Harbor, exchanging supplies under cover of night.

She rode to the ridge herself this time, no parley flag.

"You lied to my face," she said. "The raiders are yours. You used the alliance to move against the coast while I watched the wrong horizon."

"Believe what you like," Vane said.

"I believe what my own scouts saw with their own eyes." Mira Thorne's hand did not leave the hilt of her sword. "The alliance is finished, Kestrel Vane. From this hour, you and I are enemies again, and I will meet the next raider ship at the coast myself, with everything Emberfall has left to give."

She did not wait for his answer. Word of the broken alliance reached Emberfall before she did, and this time no one in the city was surprised.
