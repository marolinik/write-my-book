2. DEFECTS FOUND IN THE EVIDENCE (executor missed or misrepresented)

3. "SUSPICIOUSLY CLEAN" ANALYSIS

This bundle is the opposite of suspiciously clean, which is its greatest strength: it preserves its own failures (935KB of MAX_PATH stderr, three full Playwright failure texts, the 5 FAIL rows kept in _results.json as D-03's positive evidence), documents two rounds of self-corrected test-artifact false positives (phase4 hardcoded order; the 8 spurious FAILs in the D-03 fix-verify root-caused to extraction-boundary bugs), and leaves two things honestly unfixed (D-16, Z13). What I would still expect and did not find: (a) worker-proof for the one agent measurement (the single VOID); (b) any UI-level verification of the Phase-A surfaces (all deferred); (c) a fresh-book, fresh-executor D-03 fix confirmation rather than re-verification on the same drilled fixture by the same executor (Round 2's new permutation partially mitigates); (d) closure evidence for D-16 or cleanup of the corrupted shared X1 fixture; (e) more than a handful of ETag spot-checks against the 1,766 mirrored objects. One structural caution: nearly all "FIXED-VERIFIED" verdicts are produced by the same party that filed the defect, on the same fixtures — the internal consistency is excellent, but independent replication exists nowhere in this bundle.

4. OVERALL IMPRESSION

The writer's-words-are-sacred core is now genuinely strong and unusually well-evidenced — but this campaign caught three separate silent-loss classes in one week and one S1 race is still open, so the correct posture is "hardened by this QA pass, not yet hardened by habit": high-7s engineering with open-defect and regression-net drag holding the functionality/reliability lens at 7.0–7.5.
