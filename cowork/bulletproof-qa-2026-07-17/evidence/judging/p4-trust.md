## 3. "Suspiciously clean" analysis

Two bundles, opposite characters. The **P4 bundle is credibly dirty**: it self-reports a worker-contention violation, voids its own timing, discloses a broken poll script, downgrades its Core verdict to PARTIAL, and files its best finding as a duplicate of D-17 rather than claiming credit. That is what honest evidence looks like. The **W6 bundle fails the suspiciously-clean test outright**: a "21/21 PASS" headline sits on top of a raw results file containing two `ok:false` entries, with the promised corrected-evidence payloads absent from both files that supposedly embed them, plus a false "byte-for-byte" restore claim and unremoved test residue. Each is individually explicable; together they show the narrative layer being polished beyond what the raw layer supports — exactly the pattern this panel exists to catch. I did apply the discount: every W6 claim I scored on is one I could see in raw JSON.
